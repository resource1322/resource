**ReSource** is a student-to-student resource-sharing platform that connects juniors who need academic resources with seniors who already have them.

From **books and notes to calculators, lab equipment, stationery, and project materials**, students can list resources they no longer need and choose to **give, sell, exchange, or lend** them.

ReSource turns unused resources into useful opportunities—helping students **save money, reduce waste, and build a culture of sharing across campuses.**

**ReSource — Resources, Reconnected.**
