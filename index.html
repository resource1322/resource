The Academic Dilemma

The resources already exist. They're just not connected.

Every semester, thousands of students buy identical books while thousands of seniors let theirs collect dust.

01

Expensive Resources

New students spend thousands of rupees on single-use textbooks, engineering drafting tools, and calculators for 1-2 semesters.

02

Wasted Material

Seniors pass their exams and have high-quality notes, lab equipment, and question banks sitting forgotten in hostel cupboards.

03

Storage & Disposal Issues

Students graduating or shifting rooms struggle to store heavy academic gear and end up throwing away good study material.

04

No Direct Connection

Juniors don't know which senior holds the exact book or lab instrument they need, and chat messages get lost instantly.

Safety Guidelines

Campus Verification & Trust

How we keep exchanges transparent, safe, and student-focused.

College Email Verification

Every account is linked to a valid university domain address to eliminate commercial spammers.

Student Badges

See owner branch, year of study, and verified student badges before initiating a contact request.

Recommended Exchange Points

Exchanges occur in public campus zones like the library foyer, departmental canteens, or hostel gates.

100% Student Driven

ReSource never asks for bank passwords or transaction commissions. All cash or UPI transactions occur directly between students upon item inspection.
